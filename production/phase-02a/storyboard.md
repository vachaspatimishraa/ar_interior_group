# Phase 02A — Cinematic storyboard

Status: concept and production direction only. No frames in this repository are presented as rendered proof.

The sequence is a single coherent commercial-interior shell. The camera never cuts to a different room; each stage adds, refines, or lights the same architectural volume.

| Stage | Story beat | Assembly direction | Camera / visual cue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Empty commercial shell | Concrete floor, perimeter shell, structural grid, and glazing are already present. | Wide three-quarter establishing view; cool daylight and long shadows. |
| 02 | Space planning and partition framing | Partition tracks, glazed meeting-room mullions, and service-core frames rise from the floor in measured bays. | Slow forward dolly from the entry axis; framing lines lead toward the back wall. |
| 03 | Architectural walls and ceiling structure | Drywall volumes, soffit bands, ceiling trays, and door openings assemble into the planned plan. | Camera lifts slightly to reveal ceiling rhythm without an exaggerated orbit. |
| 04 | Flooring and material installation | Timber floor zones, resilient floor bands, stone reception plane, and warm wall lining resolve the shell. | Parallax across the floor plane; exposure warms gradually. |
| 05 | Workstations and meeting-room furniture | Desks, task chairs, meeting table, storage, and soft seating arrive in their final zones. | Controlled lateral slide; foreground furniture layers the same sightline. |
| 06 | Lighting and finishing details | Linear pendants, wall washers, joinery pulls, planting, and restrained accessories activate the room. | Focus moves from ceiling light to work surface to meeting room; no rapid spins. |
| 07 | Completed premium commercial workspace | All materials, furniture, glazing, and lighting are settled in one coherent composition. | Final hero frame at human eye height; balanced warm/cool architectural light. |

## Directional guardrails

- The transformation is conceptual and must never be labeled as a completed AR Interior Group project.
- Geometry is additive and plausible: no morphing into unrelated shapes, no stock-photography substitutions, and no room-to-room jump cuts.
- Brand colors remain interface and accent cues. The interior uses concrete, glass, wood, metal, fabric, and architectural lighting rather than painting every object charcoal or gold.
- Desktop composition is the primary proof. Mobile is a separate vertical camera composition of the same room, not a crop that hides the planning logic.
