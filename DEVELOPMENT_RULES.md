# Development Rules

## Client PDF Content Fidelity

1. The original AR Interior Group Work Profile PDF is the primary business-content source.
2. Do not invent or paraphrase factual/business copy without explicit approval.
3. Project names, locations, services, mission, vision, CQET and claims must match the source.
4. Visually verify OCR-corrupted text before correction.
5. Do not publish obvious editing artifacts from the PDF.
6. Never show developer provenance metadata publicly.
7. Use authentic PDF/project photography only for real work.
8. Concept images must remain clearly separated from completed-project photography.
9. If the PDF does not support a fact, omit it rather than guessing.
10. Future pages must consume centralized authoritative data instead of duplicating hardcoded copy.
